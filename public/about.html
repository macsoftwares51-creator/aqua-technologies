Company Overview

Green Energy for a Clean Future

Founded in 2015, incorporated in 2019

Aqua Solar Technologies Ltd was founded in 2015 and formally incorporated in 2019 as a Private Limited Company under the Companies Act. We provide comprehensive civil engineering, electrical, water and solar services across Kenya and beyond.

Our work spans borehole drilling, solar installations, steel tank and tower fabrication, environmental impact assessments and renewable energy solutions.
